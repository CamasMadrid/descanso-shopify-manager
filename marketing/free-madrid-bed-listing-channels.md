# Free Online Listing Channels for Beds in Madrid

**Prepared:** 15 August 2026  
**Scope:** New beds, mattresses, canapés and bed-and-mattress packs sold across the Comunidad de Madrid.

## Executive recommendation

> Start with the **first 12 channels**, not all 27. They are the places most likely to produce actual consumer discovery or enquiries. The remaining channels are useful for local-search coverage, credibility, and business-to-business visibility, but should not delay a proper product feed, real photographs, delivery terms and checkout.

The supplier reference sells **canapés, mattresses, and canapé-and-mattress packs**, with a Madrid focus. It states free delivery in Madrid capital and a postcode-based calculation for the rest of the Comunidad de Madrid.[1] Use only a delivery promise that your own operation can honour, and show it plainly in every listing.

All channels below have a free basic route according to their own listing, business-profile, or catalogue information. “Free” means no mandatory platform fee for the basic presence; it does **not** include optional promoted placement, advertising, delivery, payment-processing, or subscription upgrades. Features and account eligibility can change, so confirm the current terms before publishing.

## A practical 90-minute publishing order

First, claim/create **Google Business Profile** and prepare the basic information that all other channels need: business name, telephone/WhatsApp, delivery area, hours, returns/warranty wording, logo, and 10–15 real product photographs. Second, connect Shopify to **Google Merchant Center** and add a complete feed rather than uploading products one by one. Third, create the **WhatsApp Business catalogue** and Meta catalogue, then publish only five carefully selected products on Wallapop, Milanuncios, Facebook Marketplace (if eligible), Tablón, Locanto, Anuto and Clasf.

Every item should use the same structure: product type and model; size; price including VAT; whether it is new; what is included; delivery/installation; the actual delivery window; and a direct product or WhatsApp link. Do not promise 48-hour delivery, free removal, free delivery across the whole region, a warranty, or a trial period unless your business can fulfil exactly that promise.

Before using supplier photos, descriptions, trademarks or product names, obtain permission from the supplier or confirm that your resale arrangement includes marketing-use rights. Avoid copying the competitor’s website copy or presenting their shop/location as yours.
